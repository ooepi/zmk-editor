The images in this folder are downscaled previews of nice!view screen designs,
made from each project's own images by scripts/gen-screen-previews.mjs.
The list of designs comes from https://github.com/whoop-t/nice-shield-collection (curated by whoop-t).

- gem.webp: nice!view Gem by M165437 (https://github.com/M165437/nice-view-gem). Copyright (c) 2024 Michael Schmidt-Voigt.
- battery.webp, battery-layer.webp: nice!view Battery by infely (https://github.com/infely/nice-view-battery). Copyright (c) 2024 Oleksandr Vasyliev.
- elemental.webp, elemental-2.webp: nice!view Elemental by kevinpastor (https://github.com/kevinpastor/nice-view-elemental). Copyright (c) 2024 Kevin Pastor.
- press-start.webp: nice!view Press Start by Ziembski (https://github.com/Ziembski/nice-view-press-start). Copyright (c) 2025 Ziembski.
- bongo-cat.webp: Bongo Cat with FURIOUS mode by dsifry (https://github.com/dsifry/nice-view-mod). Copyright (c) 2024 GPeye.
- spacemarine.webp, spacemarine-2.webp: WH40K Space Marine insignia by Jestar342 (https://github.com/Jestar342/nice-shield-spacemarine). Copyright (c) 2025 whoop-t.
- one-punch-ok.webp: One Punch Man OK by whoop-t (https://github.com/whoop-t/nice-one-punch-ok). Copyright (c) 2025 whoop-t.
- adventure-time.webp: Adventure Time by whoop-t (https://github.com/whoop-t/nice-adventure-time). Copyright (c) 2024 Michael Schmidt-Voigt.
- futurama-sus.webp: Futurama: Fry squint by whoop-t (https://github.com/whoop-t/nice-futurama-sus). Copyright (c) 2024 Michael Schmidt-Voigt.
- fry-button-miss.webp: Futurama: Fry misses the button by whoop-t (https://github.com/whoop-t/nice-fry-button-miss). Copyright (c) 2025 whoop-t.
- luffy-wanted.webp: One Piece: Luffy wanted poster by whoop-t (https://github.com/whoop-t/nice-luffy-wanted). Copyright (c) 2025 whoop-t.
- luffy-gear-five.webp: One Piece: Luffy Gear Five by whoop-t (https://github.com/whoop-t/nice-luffy-gear-five). Copyright (c) 2025 whoop-t.
- hammerbeam.webp, hammerbeam-2.webp: Hammerbeam slideshow by GPeye (https://github.com/GPeye/hammerbeam-slideshow). Copyright (c) 2024 GPeye.
- urchin.webp: Urchin corro animation by GPeye (https://github.com/GPeye/urchin-peripheral-animation). Copyright (c) 2024 GPeye.
